A FEW THINGS YOU MAY BE WONDERING

Ask me anything

DO YOU HELP WITH POSING?

Yes. We’ll guide you through posing and movement. You don’t need modeling experience—or to know what to do with your hands.

WHAT SHOULD WE WEAR?

We provide outfit and styling guidance based on the setting, season, and kind of session you have in mind.

CAN WE BRING OUR PETS?

Yes, for suitable sessions and pet-friendly locations. Tell us about your animal’s needs and comfort beforehand so we can plan around them.

CAN YOU HELP US CHOOSE A LOCATION?

Yes. We can discuss location styles, light, accessibility, and any permission or location fees before you decide.

DO YOU PHOTOGRAPH SURPRISE PROPOSALS?

Yes. We offer photography and practical timing and location guidance. More involved planning, decorations, props, or third-party vendors need a separate quote. We aren’t a full-service event-planning company.

DO YOU OFFER INSTANT-FILM PHOTOGRAPHS?

Optional instant-film keepsakes are available for select sessions, subject to equipment and film availability. They are physical originals alongside your digital photographs. Exposure and development can vary; we don’t promise unlimited retakes or a particular number of successful prints.

WILL OUR PHOTOGRAPHS BE POSTED ONLINE?

We’ll discuss your preferences separately from booking. Promotional use requires appropriate permission from recognizable participants, including parent or guardian permission for minors. Receiving a gallery or tagging the studio is not permission to publish.

A QUESTION OF YOUR OWN?
WRITE US A LITTLE NOTE
CAN WE BOOK ON A BUDGET?

We’re developing different session lengths and occasional special-rate opportunities. Ask us about the available options. Prices will be published once the launch packages are confirmed.

WHEN WILL WE RECEIVE OUR PHOTOGRAPHS?

We’ll confirm the editing and delivery timeframe in your booking documents before you reserve. A standard turnaround has not yet been published.

HOW DO WE RESERVE OUR DATE?

An inquiry starts a conversation. Your date is reserved only once availability is confirmed and the required booking agreement and payment are completed. Inquiring alone does not reserve a date.

DO YOU OFFER WEDDINGS?

Not currently. We focus on couples, engagements, proposals, families, pets, and portraits. We do not offer wedding-day photography or wedding videography.

DO YOU TRAVEL?

Tell us your city or preferred location when you inquire. We’ll confirm whether we can travel there and any associated fee before booking. Our standard service area is being finalized.

WHAT IF IT RAINS?

Weather and rescheduling terms will be provided with the booking agreement before you reserve. We aren’t publishing a rain-date promise until those terms are finalized.
